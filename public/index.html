Every personalized magazine is custom-made to order. Design takes 3 business days, printing takes 2 business days after design approval, and shipping usually takes 6 to 7 business days.

Orders can be canceled within 2 hours of placement. Once custom design work begins, cancellation is no longer available. Replacements are available for verified transit damage or production defects.
